Opportunities

 

Opportunities are always available to be part of the PHSBronco97 Reunion Committee. Passionate members help achieve the ultimate reunion experience through a diverse range of talents and skill sets:

Account & Budget – generate advance cash flow, manage budget and payout.
Communication – build and manage information channels to classmates.
Entertainment – select creative and book ideal entertainment offerings.
Event Logistics – plan and manage the overall day of experience.
Food & Beverage – select and secure quality food & beverage offerings.
Supply Procurement – acquire, set & strike, and manage supplies needed for the events.
Venue Procurement – research and secure the best venue for events.

Business connections and contributions are also welcomed for party supplies & logistics, food & beverage, entertainment, photographer, and travel & accommodations.

Please email parkroseclassof1997@outlook.com if you would like to lead any great opportunities.
