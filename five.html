Welcome to our Five Year Reunion!

 

On June 13, 2002, just nine days before the official anniversary of our graduation, alumni of the Parkrose High School Class of 1997 gathered to celebrate our Five-Year Reunion.

The event was held at the McMenamins Edgefield Power Station Pub in Troutdale, Oregon. The casual venue provided the perfect atmosphere for classmates to reconnect, allowing guests to come and go throughout the evening at their leisure.

Starting at 7:00 p.m. and continuing late into the night, alumni traveled from near and far to catch up with old friends, share memories, and create new ones. Specialty drinks, casual dining, photos, and great conversations made for an enjoyable and memorable evening.

Hosted by the PHSBronco97 Reunion Committee, reunion announcements were shared through classmates.com, and word-of-mouth from fellow alumni. A special thank you to everyone who helped spread the word and contributed to the success of the event.

Thank you to all who attended and helped make our Five-Year Reunion such a memorable occasion. We look forward to seeing everyone again at our next class gathering!

Best Wishes,
PHSBronco97 Reunion Committee
