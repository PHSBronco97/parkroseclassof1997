Welcome to our Ten Year Reunion!

 

More than 60 members of the Parkrose High School Class of 1997 came together for a special two-day "Back-to-School" reunion celebration on June 23-24, 2007.

The festivities began Saturday evening at 6:00 P.M. in the Lodge Room at Gateway Elks Lodge. Alumni and guests arrived to an atmosphere filled with Parkrose pride, surrounded by the familiar green, black, and white school colors. Senior awards adorned the walls, while original editions of The Equestrian newspaper from our high school years were displayed throughout the room, providing a nostalgic trip down memory lane.

The evening featured a catered buffet dinner with Chicken Parmesan, Pacific Halibut, scalloped potatoes, rice pilaf, and steamed vegetables. A fully stocked bar served as a gathering place where classmates reconnected, shared stories, and reminisced about their high school days.


Entertainment throughout the night included both formal and informal activities, highlighted by an open microphone session hosted by Ari Karczag. Alumni speakers Andy Over, Allisun Simpson, and Adam Harrison shared reflections and memories from their Parkrose experiences. Jim Pham also paid tribute to classmates serving in the United States Armed Forces and led a moment of remembrance honoring three classmates who had passed away.

One of the evening's most anticipated highlights was the debut of the long-awaited Senior Slide Show. Attendees enjoyed a special preview featuring treasured high school photographs and memories. Each alumnus received a commemorative DVD containing the complete Senior Slide Show along with additional bonus features, preserving nearly an hour of unforgettable moments from our school years.

The evening concluded with a class photo before classmates took to the dance floor to celebrate, reconnect, and enjoy the remainder of the night together.



Sunday's activities began at 11:30 A.M. at Parkrose High School. Despite Portland's characteristic rainy weather, the school cafeteria and gymnasium provided an ideal setting for a family-friendly gathering. Alumni, spouses, partners, and children enjoyed catered food and refreshments while participating in a variety of activities, including football, basketball, Ultimate Frisbee, trivia games, and plenty of opportunities to catch up with old friends.

After more than a year of planning and volunteer efforts, the PHSBronco97 Reunion Committee would like to extend its sincere appreciation to everyone who attended and helped make our Ten-Year Reunion such a memorable success. Based on the positive feedback and wonderful turnout, we are grateful to have shared this special weekend with so many classmates and friends.


Thank you for a fantastic Ten-Year Reunion. We look forward to seeing everyone again at the next class gathering!
Sincerely, PHSBronco97 Reunion Committee
