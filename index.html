We’re the Broncos! Rooted in Portland, Oregon, our class represents a unique journey through the Parkrose School District. Many of us began our educational adventure at Prescott, Sacramento, Shaver, or Sumner Elementary schools before coming together at Parkrose Middle School and ultimately graduating from Parkrose High School. From kindergarten through senior year, our paths crossed, friendships formed, and memories were made within a community that shaped who we are today.

Like every graduating class, we shared the triumphs, challenges, milestones, and unforgettable moments that define the high school experience. We also hold a special place in Parkrose history as the first class to graduate from the newly constructed Parkrose High School, leaving behind the aging building that served us through most of our high school years.

Today, decades later, the Class of 1997 continues to celebrate a lifetime of experiences, achievements, friendships, and memories. This website serves as both a digital time capsule and a connection hub for classmates, family, and friends. Whether you're here to reminisce, reconnect, share life updates, explore our shared history, or celebrate the journey we've taken since graduation, we hope you'll find something meaningful along the way.

Thank you for visiting, and welcome back to the place where our story began. Onto the end for Parkrose High!
