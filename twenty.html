20 Years Celebration

 

On June 24, 2017, more than 100 alumni and guests gathered to celebrate a remarkable milestone: the 20-Year Reunion of the Parkrose High School Class of 1997. Organized by the PHSBronco97 Reunion Committee, the event welcomed anyone who had been enrolled with the Class of 1997 at any point during their K-12 journey within the Parkrose School District.

A Unique Venue for Reconnecting
The reunion was held at the historic McMenamins Kennedy School, a venue perfectly suited for an evening of nostalgia and fun. Classmates explored the former school-turned-hotel, enjoying everything from a pint in an old classroom to a movie in the auditorium and conversations throughout the property's distinctive gathering spaces. The celebration centered around a private Gymnasium event space and adjoining courtyard, where guests enjoyed a warm summer evening while reconnecting with lifelong friends and former classmates.

An Evening Filled with School Spirit
High school memories were woven throughout the event. Themed centerpieces celebrated athletics, cheer and dance, performing arts, academics, and pop culture, including a playful tribute to the infamous "REDO" stamp familiar to many graduates. Upon arrival, guests were greeted by volunteers at the registration table and received custom magnetic name badges along with drink tickets. The evening officially kicked off at 7:00 P.M. with spirited cheerleader-style red carpet arrivals that set the tone for a memorable night.

Great Food, Drinks, and Conversation
A progressive menu of heavy hors d'oeuvres was available throughout the evening, featuring favorites such as meatballs, salad rolls, spanakopita, Cajun tots, deli selections, hummus, and pizza. Dessert was a reunion highlight, with cupcakes arranged to spell "1997" in celebration of the graduating class. Guests also enjoyed a private cash bar offering a variety of beers, wines, ciders, cocktails, coffee, and tea, creating the perfect backdrop for conversation, laughter, and reminiscing.

Memories Brought Back to Life
Throughout the evening, alumni revisited cherished memories through interactive displays and activities. Memory tables showcased Equestrian newspapers, high school apparel, team photographs, and yearbooks spanning freshman through senior years. Additional entertainment included conversation cards, cornhole, drinking games, and a photo booth complete with reunion-themed props, providing countless opportunities to reconnect and create new memories.

Honoring the Past and Celebrating the Present
The formal program was hosted by Jim Pham and included moments of reflection, recognition, and celebration. Classmates who had passed away were honored in a special remembrance, while those who served or were serving in the United States Armed Forces received recognition for their contributions. The program also featured reunion trivia, challenging attendees to recall favorite television shows, local hangouts, student government leaders, athletic coaches, yearbook memories, and other pieces of Parkrose history. The friendly competition rewarded participants with drink vouchers and plenty of laughter.

A Picture Worth a Thousand Memories
The evening's formal presentation concluded with a class photograph, capturing the Class of 1997 together once again, two decades after graduation. The remainder of the night was spent socializing, reminiscing, and enjoying music from familiar decades. As an added keepsake, each attendee received a USB wristband containing a digitized version of the Parkrose High School Class of 1997 yearbook, ensuring that a piece of history could be taken home and preserved for years to come.

As another decade passes, the PHSBronco97 Reunion Committee extends its heartfelt appreciation to every classmate who attended and supported this celebration. Whether reconnecting with lifelong friends, sharing memories, or simply enjoying the opportunity to gather again, each attendee helped make the reunion a meaningful and memorable event.

Thank You, Class of 1997
Thank you for making our Twenty-Year Reunion an extraordinary success. We look forward to seeing everyone again at the next reunion.

Sincerely, PHSBronco97 Reunion Committee
